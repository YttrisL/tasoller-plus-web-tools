Reads the microcontroller of the controller through the USB command channel: the bootloader and both firmware slots, the settings, the configuration words, the RAM, and the registers as the bootloader left them. Available with the Supernova custom firmware only.

1. Device

Device: not connected Firmware version: -

2. Dump

Nothing is written to the device. The controller keeps working while it is read. Dump everything at once (about a minute), or only one section with the Dump button of its row.

The zip holds one .bin file per section that was read, manifest.json and info.txt. The unique chip ID identifies your unit: keep it hidden before you share a dump.
